## 1. Overview

Carsloox: Parking Puzzle is a free mobile puzzle game. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the app and how it is used.

The app does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or contacts to play. Game progress, coins, unlocked items, selected backgrounds, and sound settings may be stored locally on your device.

- IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
- app and advertising interactions, such as launches, taps, impressions, and video completion;
- diagnostic and performance information; and
- device or advertising identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or App Set ID where applicable.

This information may be used for delivering and measuring ads, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Data sent by Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete the Android Advertising ID through your Android device settings.

For more information, see [Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
